Privacy Rights Online

What Counts as Personal Data?

Personal data is information that can identify a person directly or indirectly. Names, email addresses, device IDs, location history, browsing behavior, photos, and account activity can all reveal something about a user.

How Platforms Collect Data

Websites and apps collect data through sign-up forms, cookies, location permissions, shopping histories, likes, shares, searches, and time spent viewing content. The proposal focuses on helping users understand this process before they agree to it.

Common Digital Rights

  • The right to know what data is being collected
  • The right to access or correct personal information
  • The right to request deletion in certain situations
  • The right to stronger security and clearer consent

Why Students Should Care

Students use learning systems, social platforms, streaming apps, and AI tools for school and daily life. Understanding privacy rights helps them make better choices about settings, consent, sharing, and online safety.

Research source: Federal Trade Commission Privacy and Security.

Key Terms

Consent: Permission for data to be collected or used.

Personal data: Information linked to an identifiable person.

Data broker: A company that collects or sells information about people.
