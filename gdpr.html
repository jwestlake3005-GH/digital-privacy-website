The General Data Protection Regulation

What Is GDPR?

The General Data Protection Regulation, or GDPR, is a major European Union privacy law. It sets rules for how organizations collect, store, explain, protect, and share personal data.

Major User Rights

  • Access personal data a company has about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion in some cases
  • Object to certain uses of data
  • Receive clear explanations about data practices

How It Affects Online Platforms

Platforms must think carefully about consent, privacy notices, security, data sharing, and how long user information is kept. GDPR also influences global companies because many online services operate across borders.

GDPR at a glance
PrincipleMeaning for Users
TransparencyCompanies should explain what data they collect and why.
Data minimizationCompanies should collect only what they need.
SecurityCompanies should protect personal data from misuse.

Research source: European Commission Data Protection in the EU.

Quick Fact

GDPR became enforceable on May 25, 2018, and remains one of the most influential privacy laws in the world.
