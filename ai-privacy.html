AI, Data Collection, and Online Safety

How AI Uses Data

AI systems can depend on large amounts of data to train models, personalize recommendations, detect abuse, or automate decisions. This creates privacy questions about consent, retention, transparency, and whether people understand how their information is being used.

Risks for Users

  • Personal information may be used in ways users did not expect.
  • Automated decisions can affect visibility, accounts, or opportunities.
  • AI tools can create convincing misinformation or impersonation.
  • Privacy notices may be difficult for everyday users to understand.

Vocabulary

Automated decision: A decision made partly or fully by software.

Data retention: How long information is kept.

Transparency: Clear explanation of how systems work and use data.
